## Return Policy

### Q1. What items are eligible for return?
- Returns are accepted within 14 days of the order's delivery date.
- Items must be in original condition: unworn, unwashed, with all original labels attached.
- Returns are not accepted, even within 14 days, if:
  1. The product was used for anything other than trying it on.
  2. The product is damaged, stained, or has odors after delivery.
  3. Accompanying items are missing (accessories, attachments, invoices, or product tags).
  4. The product is an undergarment, sock, or tights that touches the skin, unless it is defective or there was a shipping error.
  5. The product is a sale, outlet, customized, or marked item, unless it is defective or there was a shipping error.
  6. The product was bought somewhere other than this online store.
  7. The product is not official merchandise, or was bought from sources other than the official online store.

### Q2. What fees apply for returns?
- Defective item: full refund, and the company covers return shipping. Return the item using the shipping label the company issues.
- Customer-initiated return (for example, item not as expected, incorrect size, accidental order): the original shipping fee is not refunded, and the customer pays the return shipping.
- Items that are not eligible under the policy, or that were not intended for return, will not be refunded. Such items that were sent back are returned to the customer at the customer's expense.
- Canadian customers receive a full refund of duties and taxes on returned orders, in line with CBSA requirements.

### Q3. I received a faulty item. How can I return it?
- Contact customer service with photos of the faulty item and your order number. The photos help the company address the issue with its shipping partner.
- After the company receives the photos and confirms the issue, it starts the return and refund process.

### Q4. An item is missing from my order.
- Tell customer service which products are missing, and include a photo of the delivery package so they can investigate.

### Q5. How long does the refund process take?
- Refunds can take up to 21 business days to be credited to the original payment method. This covers shipping time to the warehouse, processing and inspection, and finance department approval.
- After finance processes the refund, funds may take up to 7 business days to appear in your account.
- If no refund arrives within 21 business days, provide the tracking number of the returned package so the company can investigate.
